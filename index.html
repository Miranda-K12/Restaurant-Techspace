Booking FAQs

How can I book a table at a restaurant?

You can book a table by calling the restaurant directly, using their website, or through reservation apps like OpenTable.

What information is required when making a reservation?

you need to provide your name, contact details, number of guests, date, and time of your visit.

Can I modify or cancel my reservation?

Yes, most restaurants allow modifications or cancellations, but you should do so in advance according to their cancellation policy.

Is it necessary to confirm a reservation?

yes, require confirmation via phone, SMS, or email to ensure the booking is valid.

What happens if I arrive late for my reservation?

Restaurants may hold your table for a limited time (usually 10–15 minutes). After that, they may give it to other customers.

Can I walk in without a reservation?

Yes, walk-ins are usually welcome, but availability depends on how busy the restaurant is at that time.
